Frequently Asked Questions

What theological framework does GraceTools use?

You choose. Select your doctrinal position — Roman Catholic, Eastern Orthodox, Anglican, Lutheran, Reformed, Baptist, Methodist, Pentecostal/Charismatic, Non-Denominational Evangelical, Acts 2 Dispensational, or Mid-Acts Dispensational — and the AI frames the passage, its audience, and its application accordingly.

Do credits expire?

No. Credits you purchase never expire — use them whenever you need them.

What if a sermon fails to generate?

If generation fails for any reason, your credits are automatically refunded in full — you're never charged for a failed attempt.

Can I upload my own notes, a PDF, or an audio recording?

Yes. The builder supports typed text, a Bible passage or topic, a YouTube link, an uploaded PDF, or an uploaded audio recording that gets transcribed automatically.

What is the Fact Checker?

It's an optional add-on that runs a second AI pass over your generated sermon, checking scripture accuracy, flagging fabricated claims, and confirming doctrinal consistency. It only flags concerns for your review — it never rewrites anything automatically.

What Bible translations are supported?

KJV, NASB, NKJV, ESV, and NIV are currently available in the builder.

How long does generation take?

Most sermons generate and arrive by email within 30 to 90 seconds.

What's the difference between Essentials, Preacher, and Master packages?

Essentials includes a title, big idea, main points, and key takeaways. Preacher adds a full introduction, historical/cultural background, cross-references, and conclusion. Master adds verse-by-verse commentary, character studies, a children's message, and discussion questions.
