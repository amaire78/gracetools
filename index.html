          Questions

          Who owns the book?

          You do, completely. It's built from your material. Publish it, sell it, put your name on it.

          How long does it take?

          The sample comes back in minutes. The full book takes about an hour, and you watch it chapter by chapter.

          Will it invent things I never said?

          Every chapter is written strictly from your sources, then checked against them for unsupported claims. Where your sources disagree, we address the disagreement rather than quietly picking a side.

          What happens to my files?

          Private storage used only for your book, permanently deleted the moment it's finished.

          What if I don't like the sample?

          Two free rewrites. If it's still not right, walk away — you were never charged.
