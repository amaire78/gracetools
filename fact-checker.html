Fact Checker: A Second Set of Eyes on Every Citation

Every sermon is generated by one AI model. The Fact Checker runs a second, different-family AI over it to catch scripture and doctrinal errors the first model might reproduce or miss.

Add Fact Checker to a Sermon →

Why it matters

AI models can make confident mistakes — a misquoted verse, a reference that doesn't say what it's cited to say, a doctrinal claim that doesn't hold up. Checking a model's work with the same model tends to repeat its blind spots. The Fact Checker uses a different model family specifically so it catches what the first one missed — before Sunday morning, not during it.

What it checks

  • Scripture citations — does the reference say what the sermon claims it says
  • Doctrinal consistency — does the content align with your selected doctrinal position
  • Clear severity ratings on anything flagged, in plain English

How it works

  1. Generate any sermon package (Essentials, Preacher, or Master)
  2. Add Fact Checker as an add-on before or after generation
  3. Review flagged items with severity and plain-English explanations directly in the sermon viewer
